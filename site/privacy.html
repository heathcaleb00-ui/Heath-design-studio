Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026.

Heath Design Studio is run by Caleb Heath in the Dallas–Fort Worth area. This page explains what information this website collects and how it's used.

What's collected

When you submit the quote form, you send the details you enter: your name, business name, email address, project type, approximate budget, current website, and project description. If you email or call, I receive whatever you choose to share.

How it's used

  • To reply to your request and prepare a quote.
  • To communicate with you about a project you've asked about.

Your information is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone for marketing.

Where it's stored

The site is hosted on Netlify. Form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify and sent to my email inbox. Netlify may also keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Your choices

To ask what information I have from you, or to have it deleted, email heathcaleb00@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

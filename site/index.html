FAQ

Straight answers.

Don't see your question? Ask it in the quote form or call (214) 994-2239.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content and feedback come together. A focused Launch Website generally moves faster than a larger Business Website. Your quote includes an estimated timeline.

How does pricing and payment work?

Website projects are a one-time fee, starting at the prices listed above. Website Care and Local Growth are billed monthly. Your written quote sets out the final price and payment schedule before any work starts.

Will I own my website?

Your domain and your business content stay yours. Ownership of the finished website is written into your project agreement, so it's clear before we begin.

What about my domain name and hosting?

If you already have a domain, we'll use it. If not, I can help you register one in your business's name. Domain registration is paid directly to the registrar.

Sites are hosted on Netlify, and I handle the setup and connection to your domain.

Do I need an ongoing maintenance plan?

No, it's optional. Website Care ($99/month) is there if you'd like updates and regular checks handled for you. Without a plan, you can still reach out when you need changes.

Will my site rank on Google?

Every site is built with solid SEO foundations, but no one can honestly guarantee rankings. Search results depend on your competition, content, reviews, and time. Local Growth is for businesses that want ongoing work on local search.
